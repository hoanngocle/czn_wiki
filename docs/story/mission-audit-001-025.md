## Result

No formally issued Vận Mệnh / Định Mệnh quest occurs in chapters 1–25. There are explicit non-system requests/work below, plus a genuine system mission recalled from the previous life. They must not be presented as newly issued War God quests.

### A. Khống chế công suất của tay lái dầu điện tử số 2

- **Title:** exact research-topic wording, not invented quest title.
- **Kind / issuer:** registered research work; Cố Thanh Sơn proposes it, the central computer of Bộ phận nghiên cứu và phát triển Chiến giáp Trường Ninh approves it.
- **Start:** chapter 11 ¶15–19. “Số hiệu 41175, xin nghiên cứu đề tài.” / “Khống chế công suất của tay lái dầu điện tử số 2.” / “[Số hiệu 41157, xin phép của anh đã được duyệt, thời gian là sáu ngày. Phòng ký túc của anh là 4009.]” The differing employee numbers are present in the source.
- **Objective:** carry out the named power-control research topic.
- **Time / benefit:** permission lasts six days; room 4009 is granted. ¶19 says completing the topic well could earn “phần thưởng tương ứng”; no quantity is specified. Do not invent a system reward or failure penalty.
- **Updates:** chapter 11 ¶55 ends a day's research. Chapter 18 ¶35 says “Hắn chỉ mất khoảng năm phút đã giải quyết xong những vấn đề nghiên cứu”; likely related to this workplace research, but the topic is not repeated, so completion linkage is an inference. Chapter 19 ¶15–18 explicitly terminates employment: “[Rời khỏi ngay hôm nay, anh đã bị đuổi việc.]” No formal topic acceptance/payment scene occurs here.

### B. Làm quà sinh nhật Thiên Thần Rực Lửa cho Tô Tuyết Nhi (editorial title)

- **Kind / issuer:** personal request, Tô Tuyết Nhi; accepted by Cố Thanh Sơn. Not a War God interface quest.
- **Start:** chapter 17 ¶23–24: “Tuần sau là sinh nhật em, em muốn có quà sinh nhật!” / Cố Thanh Sơn responds “Được.”
- **Objective:** make her birthday present. Cố Thanh Sơn himself chooses an advanced combat robot; she initially understands it to be a model (¶207). Asked about shape, she says “Em thích Thiên Thần Rực Lửa” (¶211), and he says he will make the present (¶214).
- **Time:** birthday is next week; this is the request's temporal context, not a system countdown.
- **Progress:** chapter 17 ¶55–75 he requests Nữ Thần Công Chính's materials/technical assistance and pays 2,000 merit points from a total 2,002.41. This payment is a cost, not a penalty. ¶97–114 work begins; ¶187 all technical problems resolved after seven hours; ¶218 starts the first model; ¶223–229, four hours later, “Chiến giáp cơ động Thiên Thần Rực Lửa đã hoàn thành” and is with Nữ Thần Công Chính for further testing before direct transport to Tô Tuyết Nhi. ¶229 explicitly says “Bây giờ đã làm xong quà sinh nhật”. Product manufacture is complete; physical handoff is not established within chapter 25.
- **Later consequence:** chapter 24 ¶29–37 Nữ Thần Công Chính rates the research “Hoàn mỹ”, “Vượt thời đại”, sets **total** personal merit to **799873957281439**, and raises access to **Lãnh tụ tối cao**. These are civic research-achievement benefits, not a specified reward promised by Tô Tuyết Nhi or a War God quest. ¶33 preserves the oral confidentiality agreement. Chapter 25 ¶2–6 authorizes a demonstration to the president and general.
- **No explicit failure penalty.**

### C. Đưa Triệu Lục cùng thoát khỏi doanh trại (editorial title; weak candidate)

- **Kind / issuer:** plea for rescue from Triệu Lục, not a formally accepted assignment.
- **Start:** chapter 2 ¶74: “Thân thể ngươi có tốt lên chút nào không? Có thể mang ta cùng trốn đi không?” ¶76 reveals this hope motivated him to provide healing pills.
- **Response:** chapter 3 ¶1 Cố Thanh Sơn says he needs more days to recover; ¶2 Triệu Lục tells him to rest, disappointed. No explicit promise, quest acceptance, reward contract, deadline, or completion here.
- **Related survival events:** chapter 4 ¶43 concealment would fail within two days; chapter 14 ¶15 finding 75 spirit stones extends concealment more than ten days. These are changing environmental constraints, not a quest deadline. Cố Thanh Sơn feeds and protects Triệu Lục in chapters 12–15, but they have not escaped by chapter 25. Check the next audit range for a possible later escape.
- **Recommendation:** if the UI includes personal requests, label as a request, not a system mission. If it requires accepted assignments, exclude.

### D. Tiến đến Ma Chủ cuối cùng (editorial title; past-life system mission)

- **Kind / issuer:** real mission of the old game's system, recalled from the previous life; not a new mission from the War God interface.
- **First narrated:** chapter 3 ¶17: “Chỉ có Cố Thanh Sơn dẫn dắt Công hội Quốc gia, hoàn thành được nhiệm vụ mà hệ thống giao, thành công đánh tới trước mặt tên Ma Chủ cuối cùng.” Exact assignment wording/title and issue time are not provided. It says the mission was completed and they reached the final Demon Lord; do not assume a formal objective more specific than that.
- **Continuation:** ¶18 allies have sacrificed themselves and he knocks the lord from Vạn Cốt Nhục Tọa; ¶19–20 humanity loses the overall campaign; ¶23 he strikes the lord's heart. ¶25 system confirms he killed the final Ma Chủ and grants “ban thưởng lần chót”. The text does not explicitly identify that last reward as the reward of the mission in ¶17.
- **Reward connection:** ¶26 receives an object; ¶32–48 identifies/examines the golden short sword, which dissolves into him; ¶50 says “Kích hoạt thành công, giao diện thao tác chiến thần đã mở.” Can describe narrative chain, but avoid claiming that the previously unnamed mission was formally titled “Giết Ma Chủ cuối cùng”.
- **Recommendation:** include only in an explicitly marked past-life/recalled category if scope includes all missions mentioned.

### E. Pha rượu để báo đáp Anna (editorial title; past-life personal request)

- **Kind / issuer:** Anna, answering Cố Thanh Sơn's offer to repay her life-saving favor; explicitly previous life.
- **Evidence:** chapter 20 ¶20–25: years after she saved him, he asks how to repay her; she says “anh muốn đền ơn thì không bằng pha cho tôi một loại rượu ngon.” He learns rare secret mixing methods, returns, but “cô cũng đã không còn nữa rồi.”
- **Status:** past-life request not delivered before her death. Chapter 20 ¶46–75 current-life cocktails Cung Bọ Cạp and Liệt Diễm Địa Ngục reflect that remembered debt. Do not mark as an explicit system completion; no system, deadline, promised reward, or penalty.
- **Recommendation:** normally omit from active mission list, retain only if recalled personal requests are explicitly in scope.

## Borderline exclusions after reading context

- Chapter 1 ¶39,46–47 mentions presumed mission/reward after killing Bác Bì Huyết Ma. This is Cố Thanh Sơn's mistaken expectation while the interface is still absent; no mission title, issuance, or confirmation. Chapter 2 ¶21–24 confirms escape from apocalypse and identity setup, not a newly issued quest. Chapter 4 ¶7 rewards the kill with soul power, ordinary kill gain.
- Chapters 4,12–16 hunting, burying bodies, finding weapons/spirit stones, raising cultivation, obtaining Yến Song Phi and creating Loạn Vũ are self-directed survival/training actions. Chapter 12 ¶18 proposes burial to Triệu Lục; ¶42 the giant devours the corpses before burial. No external assignment to Cố Thanh Sơn. Chapter 16 ¶26 is general skill-training encouragement, not a quest.
- Chapters 5–10 and 21–24 threats, self-defense, and Nhiếp Vân's assassination orders are not missions assigned to Cố Thanh Sơn. He is their target. Chapter 7 ¶77's order to leave and cease contact is a coercive offer he rejects; chapter 19 ¶31 similar warning from Tô phu nhân is not accepted work. Police orders to remain still in chapter 21 ¶6,39 likewise are arrest commands.
- Chapter 3 ¶63–64 previous-life duties as guild commander are general employment context, not identifiable separate quests. Chapter 17 ¶65–71 lists historical merit-bearing acts (articles, rescue attempts, competition, etc.), not formal current quests.
- Chapters 10 ¶32–45,11 ¶35–44,17 ¶239–245,18,20 ¶79,21 ¶25–37: Anna/Phùng Hoắc Đức conduct their own recruiting and investigation missions. They are not assigned to Cố Thanh Sơn. Anna offers him a barony/recruitment in chapter 21 ¶10; he does not accept here.
- Chapter 12 ¶111–114 demon army pursuit/delivery orders belong to enemy forces; Cố Thanh Sơn intercepts the message. Not his mission.
- Chapter 17 ¶165–184 SSS protective military orders and ¶219 “Nhiệm vụ hoàn thành” belong to Nữ Thần Công Chính's deployed forces. They are not a formal CTS quest or completion of the birthday request. Chapter 24 protection plan likewise belongs to the AI/president/general.
- Chapter 18 ¶39–48 invitation to Tô family's reception and chapter 24 ¶14,58–75 presidential summons are meetings, not mission objectives. Chapter 25 ¶20 recruiting instruction is assigned to Trương Tông Dương's contacts, not CTS.
- Gifts of healing pills, a safe apartment, and civic protection are not automatically mission rewards; no promised contract unless explicitly recorded above.
