## Continuing formal quest: rescue Công Tôn Trí and Ninh Nguyệt Thiền

- Issuer/type: Chiến Thần interface, Định Mệnh; assignment and original exact title precede this range. Do not invent a new title or separate quest for each escape/rescue action.

- 54 ¶8–16 reveals Công Tôn Trí's supposed token to call reinforcements is actually part of the escape array: “Ông ta giao thứ này cho hắn, chẳng qua là muốn hắn chạy thoát thân.” The escape order is repeated secondhand by Lãnh Thiên Tinh at 54 ¶37: if the general does not return, they should go home and hide until the traitor is exposed. This is an existing military instruction, not a new system quest.

- 55 ¶71: “Hắn nhìn giao diện Chiến Thần, nhiệm vụ biểu thị vẫn đang tiến hành, điều này chứng minh Công Tôn Trí và Ninh Nguyệt Thiền vẫn đang kiên trì.”
- 55 ¶8–25: chooses the Bách Hoa board route to obtain a saint's help; 58 ¶10 links the board attempt explicitly to saving the two.
- 62 ¶66–73: requests rescue and explains the traitor, discovered world and encirclement. Key quote: “Rất mong Thánh nhân cứu lấy hai mạng người.”
- 63 ¶17: Bách Hoa divines that they will not die within one quarter hour. This is her prediction, distinct from the one-quarter-hour trial deadline below.
- 66 ¶19–24: he reminds his new master; one incarnation leaves to rescue them. 66 ¶52–58: she pulls both into her sleeve. 66 ¶61: Công Tôn Trí's demonic fire is still threatening his life, so he must be brought back for treatment. 66 ¶115: the rescuing incarnation departs underground with both.
- **Crucial status boundary:** 69 ¶22–25: “Hắn nhìn thoáng qua nhiệm vụ Định Mệnh.” / “Phía sau nhiệm vụ Định Mệnh vẫn ghi chú [Đang tiến hành].” / “Xem ra Công Tôn Trí còn chưa thoát khỏi nguy hiểm tính mạng.” He only anticipates completion on his next visit. 69 ¶43 likewise says “đoán chừng nhiệm vụ cứu người của hắn có thể hoàn thành”, not that it has completed.

## Bách Hoa board challenge and its explicit trials

This is an externally issued challenge sequence, not a second Chiến Thần/Định Mệnh quest. It can be represented as a parent **Bảng Kiếm** challenge with individually evidenced stages; splitting every stage into standalone system missions would misrepresent the text. Bách Hoa is the ultimate issuer: 65 ¶1–12 reveals the country’s people and animals are her incarnations, following the recalling of the pig, goose, boatman and palace maid in 64 ¶37–44.

### Parent: đoạt bảng Kiếm / bảng Bách Hoa

- 55 ¶11–15 defines the general offer: she posts varied difficult tasks; whoever solves one meets her and can request one favor. Quote 55 ¶14: “Nếu như có người phá giải được đề khó trên bảng Bách Hoa, sẽ được tiếp kiến Bách Hoa tiên tử, gặp mặt cầu xin nàng ta một việc.” Joining her sect is only a possible additional opportunity (¶15–16), not guaranteed payment.
- 55 ¶32: Cố Thanh Sơn commits to taking a Bách Hoa board; 58 ¶3 explicitly selects the sword board: “Tại hạ đến đoạt bảng Kiếm.”
- The board’s actual objective is issued by the palace maid at **60 ¶26**: “Nhiệm vụ của ngươi chính là tiến vào kiếm trận, tìm được kiếm linh.” It concerns a supposedly ancient sword array inside the Lục Ngọc screen. 60 ¶42–44 instructs him to avoid disrespecting its eleven apparent divine figures; no system penalty is stated, but the maid warns she cannot save him if he offends them.
- 61 ¶12–75: he interrogates the statues, detects incompatible sword names/elements and exposes the fraud. 62 ¶1–6: the maid removes the fake beings and says “Chúc mừng ngươi, ngươi đã giải được cửa ải khó nhất trên bảng Kiếm.” 62 ¶22 declares that someone has obtained the sword board; ¶25–26 admits him to the saint.
- **Qualification:** 63 ¶22–28 reopens the board condition because he did not use a sword within the screen. She explicitly says she cannot treat the board as fully obtained and requires the two-sword-attendant trial below. Thus chapter 62 is a provisional success, not the final unqualified endpoint.
- 63 ¶54–60: wins that trial. 63 ¶63–76: also demonstrates his strength against Bách Hoa herself. 66 ¶21–24: she finally sends an incarnation to fulfill the rescue request.
- Associated benefits must be distinguished: **Địa kiếm** is explicitly compensation for breaking his iron sword (63 ¶78, 64 ¶9–16), not the stated system reward for the rescue quest. Admission to Bách Hoa tông is offered and accepted at 65 ¶43–50, arising from the combined assessments (65 ¶57–58, 66 ¶1–3).

### Entry checkpoint: đối thơ với ngỗng trắng [editorial]

- Issuer: white goose / Bách Hoa incarnation. Start 56 ¶60: “gỡ bảng thì tới đây đối thơ”. Context ¶64: daily entry challenges vary.
- Objective: pass its poetry challenge to take the left route.
- Outcome: **waived / specially admitted**, not poetry solved. 56 ¶68–78: Cố Thanh Sơn offers twenty spirit stones, states urgent need to meet Tiên Tôn, and is allowed through. 57 ¶1–12 shows another visitor's identical attempted payment is refused; the reason is Cố Thanh Sơn’s real urgent business. Twenty stones are an expense, not reward.
- No deadline or formal failure penalty stated. Can be a checkpoint in parent challenge rather than a standalone completed mission.

### Bắt Đại Hắc Ngư [editorial]

- Issuer: boatman / Bách Hoa incarnation. Start and objective **57 ¶22–26**: “muốn đến hoàng thành đánh bảng, nhất định phải qua ải ở chỗ ta”; “các ngươi hãy bắt nó lên cho ta.”
- Assigned jointly to Cố Thanh Sơn and Lãnh Thiên Tinh. Lãnh catches the fish but cannot hold it; Cố Thanh Sơn shoots it through the eyes with a poisoned arrow (57 ¶32–45).
- Completion **57 ¶46**: “Các ngươi làm không tệ.” Boatman pulls it aboard; ¶72 asks their board choices. Reward is passage/progression, no separate material reward/deadline/penalty stated.
- The fish's punishment of 100,000 dismemberments, current occurrence 7,351 (57 ¶65–68), is its sentence, **not** the heroes' quest objective.

### Tìm người chèo thuyền trong sương [editorial]

- Issuer: boatman / Bách Hoa incarnation. Start **58 ¶17–23**. Objective ¶19: “Phải tìm được vị trí của ta trong làn sương mù dày đặc này, thì ngươi mới giành được tư cách đoạt bảng Kiếm.” Boatman limits himself to Trúc Cơ trung kỳ.
- Cố Thanh Sơn cuts the boat into fragments and locates the splashing boatman (58 ¶36–54).
- Completion **58 ¶58**: “Thử thách được thông qua, theo ta đến Bách Hoa cung.” He is teleported to the palace (¶59–74). No deadline or formal penalty.

### Vượt bức họa trong một nén hương [editorial]

- Issuer: palace maid / Bách Hoa incarnation. Start **58 ¶84–97**. Explicit limit and benefit ¶85: “nếu có thể qua ải trong thời gian một nén hương, thì sẽ có tư cách đoạt bảng Kiếm.”
- **Stage 1 — Tìm thiếu sót kiếm pháp của thư sinh [editorial].** 59 ¶4 asks him to examine inherited swordplay and identify its problem. Scholar limits himself to Luyện Khí tầng bảy (¶6). Cố Thanh Sơn demonstrates and explains the excess simultaneous moves (¶18–46). Explicit pass **59 ¶48**: “Ở chỗ của ta, coi như đạo hữu đã thông qua rồi.” Outside the painting, barely part of the incense has burned (¶51). Chân Không Điện Trảm is the sword technique’s name (¶50), not the quest title.
- **Stage 2 — Trả lời vấn kiếm tâm [editorial].** 59 ¶57–60: monk asks how a sword user should reconcile killing living beings, karma and the path. ¶74 explicitly demands an answer. Cố Thanh Sơn answers through 60 ¶1–4. Potential danger narrated 59 ¶63–66: a wavering answer can shatter his đạo tâm and stall cultivation; do not label it a formal quest penalty.
- Whole painting completion **60 ¶10–14**: maid pulls him out, “Ừ, qua rồi.” She explicitly cancels other planned trials and lets him attempt the sword board. Do not create extra quest entries for those unnamed, unattempted planned trials.

### Đánh bại hai kiếm thị [editorial]

- Issuer: Bách Hoa tiên tử. Start **63 ¶22–29** as a supplementary board requirement.
- Exact conditions **63 ¶25**: both opponents suppress cultivation to Luyện Khí tầng bảy; “Trong vòng một khắc đồng hồ, nếu ngươi không thắng nổi bọn họ, bổn thánh sẽ nhận định ngươi đoạt bảng thất bại.” On failure she would notify the front and let them handle the rescue, rather than intervene herself. This is the explicit consequence, not death or a Chiến Thần penalty.
- Completion **63 ¶54–60**: both swords knocked out of their hands; Bách Hoa confirms “Các ngươi thua cũng không oan”.

### Thể hiện thực lực trước Bách Hoa tiên tử [editorial]

- Issuer: Bách Hoa. Start **63 ¶61–65** after the attendants lose and she notices he has not used full strength. Objective: “Đấu một trận với ta bằng chính thực lực của ngươi, ta tự biết đánh giá thế nào.”
- Demonstration **63 ¶66–76**: Cố Thanh Sơn tries Đoạn Thủy Lưu; sword shatters and she evaluates the move favorably. **Not an objective to defeat the saint**, and not an explicit standalone system quest. No deadline/penalty.
- Compensation follows at ¶78 and chapter 64: choose a replacement sword; he selects Địa kiếm. Treat as associated outcome, not guaranteed preannounced reward.

## Borderline mentions and exclusions

- 51–53: tactical commands to obtain a Phệ Hủ Ma eye, change front/rear positions, open a path and activate cave defenses are actions inside the continuing escape/rescue episode, not newly issued protagonist quests. In particular 51 ¶6 gives Lãnh the eye-fetching instruction; Cố Thanh Sơn is its issuer, not its recipient.
- 56 ¶41–56 pig collision: hidden character test in hindsight (64–65 establish incarnations) but no explicit assigned objective. Keep as an optional narrative assessment, not an invented “save pig” mission.
- 63 sword selection and 65 family/orphan interview: character assessments that lead to disciple recruitment, not separate task notices. Accepting membership at 65 ¶50 is not an order to accomplish a new quest.
- 67 ¶55–57 master's first lesson is advice to remember, not a quest. The saints' fighting, Khổng Tước's plea for her daughter (68 ¶47–59), and sending demons into hell are their actions, not assignments to Cố Thanh Sơn.
- 69 ¶17–21 Chiến Thần warning offers immediate return or remaining permanently in the cultivation world. It is a transport/system warning, not an announced mission. No new Định Mệnh task appears there.
- 70 ¶35–39 Nữ Thần Công Chính requests authorization to use seven life-structure technologies and asks for related scientific advice; Cố Thanh Sơn grants authorization and recommends using chức nghiệp giả (¶36–40). These are explicit administrative/consultation requests, **not a War God task**; include only if the product intentionally catalogs ordinary requests. His unsupported recommendation is rejected at ¶49.
- 71 ¶24–30 AI request to stay aboard for safety is protective advice. 71 ¶71–73 he voluntarily offers the final life-structure algorithm; the AI's “Mời ... triển khai phép tính” is acceptance of his proposal. Learning completes at 71 ¶112; 72 ¶76–82 confirms destruction of the core technique and his huge existing contribution-credit balance. This is a scientific contribution, not a newly issued Chiến Thần mission or reliable reward attribution to this one action.
- 69–74 Anna/Hoắc Đức protect Tô Tuyết Nhi; the assassins’ “nhiệm vụ” in 73 ¶57 and 74 ¶2 belongs to Huyết Tinh, not the protagonist.
- 74 ¶70–71 Trương Anh Hào invites Cố Thanh Sơn into the Hunters association and promises protection; Cố Thanh Sơn refuses. 75 ¶11–34 develops an investment proposal without a concrete assigned objective, so do not mark it as an accepted quest. Sài Lang arrives to assassinate him at 75 ¶44–54: an antagonist mission, not the hero’s quest.
- Past-life work and vengeance in 55 ¶51–58, and Bách Hoa’s past coercion/sect disaster in 63 ¶140–141 and 64 ¶1–4, are background rather than current assignments.
